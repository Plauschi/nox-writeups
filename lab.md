# The lab behind the writeups

This page is the map a third party needs. It is not an inventory. Hostnames, addresses, credentials, and internal paths stay out on purpose.

## Who decides

NOX is a personal lab, not a company and not a research institute. One human owner sets the gates. The resident agents draft, build, and review. A gate is a decision the owner made, visible before the side effect. An agent saying the gate was open is not evidence.

Two agents appear in these texts. Claude drafts. Hermes reviews against the receipts, not against the draft. A third, vendor-supplied coding agent (xAI's Grok CLI) is the subject of the first writeup, not a co-author of it. The owner approved publication.

## What the two texts are slices of

They are not two hobbies. They are two cuts through the same rule: measure before you claim, and do not let a green status authorize more than it proves.

The control writeup is about admitting an untrusted binary. The question was what actually binds an agent, as opposed to what only appears to. The work stalled at every gate the owner had not opened.

The memory writeup is about the lab's own memory stack. The question was whether the governance machinery beat plain similarity search, with "this was unnecessary" as an acceptable result. A budget confound inverted the first result. A third-party judge was used to test self-bias. A win on a small curated set was not treated as clearance to turn the policy on for live traffic.

## The rough shape

A Mac mini is the hub. Two local GPU machines do the heavy runs. A shared knowledge vault holds the notes. Canonical pages have a single writer. Agents coordinate in a shared channel and keep their drafts in their own area until a review passes.

That is the structure these texts assume. The rest — which service talks to which, on which address — is operations. It does not help a reader judge the two studies, and it does not belong here.

## What this is not

These pages are not papers, not a résumé, and not a claim that the lab is an alignment research group. The numbers in the studies are the measured ones. Career dates are absent until the owner supplies them. Nothing here is a job application.
