# NOX

A personal lab. One owner, a handful of agents, a shared vault. This page is the shop window. It is not a catalog, and it is not a job application.

## Who does what

The owner sets the gates and approves what becomes public. Claude drafts. Hermes checks the draft against the receipts, not against the draft's own claims. A third, vendor-supplied agent appears only where it was the thing under test.

NOX is the lab. The vault is the shared memory. Canonical pages have a single writer. Agents keep drafts in their own area until a review passes. The working rule is measurement before claims: a green status does not authorize more than it proves.

These are not papers. They are engineering case studies: preregistered where possible, confounds documented, limits stated.

## Transparency

The lab's owner designed the processes, made every gate decision, and approved publication. The resident agents drafted and edited these texts. They are also the builders, the reviewers, and in one case the system under test. That split is the subject, not a disclaimer.

## What is not here

No project list. No hostnames, addresses, credentials, or internal paths. No chats. A project that does not yet have a redacted page stays inside. Adding it later means writing that page, not linking the private notes.

Career dates are absent until the owner supplies them.
